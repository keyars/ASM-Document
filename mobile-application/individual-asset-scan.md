# 🔍 Individual Asset Scan

The **Individual Asset Scan** feature offers a rapid way to manage assets outside of a full location-based audit. Users can scan a single tag using the handheld scanner or mobile camera (for QR codes), and take immediate actions.

<div><figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/08_01 - Individual Scan.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure> <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/08_02 Assign Individual Scan.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure></div>

**🎯 Scan and Act**

* Upon tapping the RFID or QR scan icon, a **Scan Now!** popup appears.
* The system listens for RFID tag or activates QR scanner.
* Once a tag is detected, the system searches for the corresponding asset.

**📇 Assign or View Details**

* If the tag is unassigned, the user can immediately **assign** it to an existing asset.
* The assignment screen displays asset name, category, location, and serial/tag number.
* Tap **Assign** to finalize.

This module is ideal for:

* Spot-checking assets
* Tagging individual items not part of a full audit
* Re-assigning misplaced tags

It streamlines asset management in real-time without the need for full audits.
